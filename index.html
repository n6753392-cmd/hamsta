In the year 2049, a rogue colony of hyper-intelligent hamsters hijacked an orbital station's coffee module. What started as a tiny rebellion became the galaxy's most beloved café chain.

At Hamsta Cafe, every brew is crafted with zero-gravity precision and served with maximum hamster enthusiasm. Our barista bots are trained by the fluffiest crew this side of the Milky Way.

We believe great coffee should feel like an event — immersive, sensory, and a little bit wild. Step into our neon-lit pods and let the hamsters fuel your next adventure.
